E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard
![Dashboard Preview](E-Commerce_Sales_Analytics_Dashboard_page-0001.jpg)

This interactive single-page Power BI dashboard was built to provide visibility into financial performance, Product brand profitabilty, and customer purchasing behavior. This model track net revenue, gross margin, and return rates across multiple channels and product categories

From the model, we can identify the peak cycle around November and December each year. The total net revenue is 36,16M with a total of 176k orders, ignoring the cancelled and returned orders. This total revenue yields a 43.80% gross margin with around 13,68% return rate. 

The highest-selling brand is Northpeak with around 6,51M with the second highest being Stonebrook. Northpeak's highest revenue comes from selling backpacks, meanwhile Stonebrook's primary product is tents, which become the highest-selling product by category compared to backpacks from Northpeak. The highest sales by channel happened on the web, followed by the app and marketplace, with the store being the least popular channel.
